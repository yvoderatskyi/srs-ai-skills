# ADR Traceability

Use the installed `domain-modeling` skill's ADR eligibility rules and `ADR-FORMAT.md` for location, numbering, and content. This reference adds only requirements traceability.

Link each ADR from its source ledger record and link back to that record from the ADR. Keep the exact decision and affected requirement/artifact IDs in the ledger, including ordinary decisions that do not qualify for an ADR.

Preserve approval evidence and distinguish a settled choice from approval of the artifact. Record pending approval accurately; when a previously accepted decision changes, preserve the old record and identify its replacement. Recording a requirements constraint does not authorize implementing an architecture change.
